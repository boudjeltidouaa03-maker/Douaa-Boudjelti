From insight to final video

  1. Research

    Audience problems, desires, behaviors and objections. Competitor and trend monitoring.

  2. Ideation

    Concepts, hooks and angles for each audience problem, tested against the objective.

  3. Scripts

    Story structure and scripts written for short-form formats such as Reels and TikTok.

  4. Production

    I brief the filmmaker, plan shoots and keep the work on strategy and on schedule.

  5. Editing and revisions

    I review cuts with the editor and manage revision rounds until the video fits the brief.

  6. Delivery and learning

    Final delivery, then a read of the analytics to inform the next round of ideas.
